Muhammad
Irslan Khalid

Dr Muhammad Irslan Khalid holds a B.S. (2013) in civil engineering from University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, an M.S. (2015) in civil engineering (Transportation engineering) from the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, and a Ph.D. (2021) in civil engineering (geotechnical engineering) from the Hanyang University. He joined the faculty of the College of Civil and Transportation Engineering at Shenzhen University in 2022. ​ He focuses on the use of advanced computational methods to solve challenging engineering problems, with primary interests in geotechnical earthquake engineering and soil-structure-interaction. His fields of expertise include probabilistic seismic hazard analysis, nonlinear site response analysis, liquefaction assessment, seismic slope stability, seismic performance of underground structure and interaction with tall buildings, lifeline performance assessment, design of offshore foundations. He is also interested in soil characterization using laboratory and field testing.
